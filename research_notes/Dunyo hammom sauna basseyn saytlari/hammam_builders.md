# World's leading Turkish hammam design-and-build companies and their websites (as of 2026-11-09)

Scope note: every homepage below was actually fetched on 2026-11-09 unless marked otherwise. Descriptions of "what the site shows" come from text/markup extraction (WebFetch), not from rendered screenshots, so visual polish (motion, typography, colour) is only partly observable. Companies whose site could not be verified are kept in a separate "unverified" list and must not be treated as recommended references.

## Who are the top hammam builders/designers, what do they do, and what have they built?

### Takeaway
The clearest hammam specialists with verifiable sites are Istanbul manufacturers (Sauna Dekor, plus the hotel fit-out group Sagist / HFC), followed by European/UK thermal-suite contractors (Barr + Wray, Topline, Effe/Effegibi) and Gulf interior fit-out firms (Antonovich Group). Juno Spa, often cited as Turkey's hammam leader, has a parked domain; Promet's site has an expired SSL certificate.

### Inferences
- There is no single dominant global "hammam brand"; the category is served by (a) Istanbul manufacturers that ship and install worldwide, (b) Western thermal-suite contractors where the hammam is one room in a spa, and (c) Gulf interior fit-out houses. A small firm competing on "authentic hammam craft" is competing mainly with category (a).
- Even large players have weak web hygiene (parked domain, expired SSL), so a technically clean, fast site is itself a differentiator.

## Which sites are genuinely impressive, which are weak, and what can a small firm copy?

### Takeaway
Sauna Dekor is the strongest hammam-specific marketing site (education + specs + interactive explainers + WhatsApp); Barr + Wray is the cleanest "premium contractor" presentation; Antonovich and Topline show how clutter and template bugs undercut luxury copy; Juno and Promet show that a dead domain or expired certificate erases years of reputation.

### Cited Findings
- Impressive: Sauna Dekor — published operating temperatures, warranty terms, ISO, a clear 4-step process, temperature-ladder navigation, project-by-city map, comparison tables, AI planner, multi-office WhatsApp — [saunadekor.com](https://saunadekor.com/)
- Impressive: Barr + Wray — restrained text-led hero, tight 3-tile segmentation, big named-hotel portfolio grid with location per card, single clear "book a call with our technical team" CTA — [barrandwray.com](https://barrandwray.com/)
- Impressive in parts: Effe — designer-name storytelling (Urquiola, Dordoni), gated hammam eBooks, steam-sizing selector, 6 languages — [effe.it](https://www.effe.it/en-us/)
- Weak: Antonovich — oversized menus, placeholder project names, template text bugs, conflicting response-time promises, unsupported "#1" claim — [antonovich-design.ae](https://antonovich-design.ae/sauna-and-hamam-design.html)
- Weak: Topline — duplicated/inconsistent menus, unrendered "{{current_year}}", no named hammam project on the hammam page — [topline.uk.net](https://www.topline.uk.net/hammam-rooms)
- Broken: Juno (domain parked at GoDaddy) and Promet (expired SSL) as of 2026-11-09 — [junospa.com fetch -> forsale.godaddy.com]; [promet.com.tr fetch error]

### Inferences (ideas a small Uzbek turnkey firm could copy in spirit)
- **"Temperature ladder" / room journey** (from Sauna Dekor): one interactive strip — pool -> hammam 40-50 C -> steam -> sauna 80-100 C — each step opening its page. Fits a firm that builds all three.
- **Publish the specs** (Sauna Dekor, Topline): göbek taşı temperature, dome height, steam generator kW per m3, build time in weeks, warranty years. Specific numbers signal craft more than adjectives.
- **Process explainer with real crew photos** (Sauna Dekor 4 steps): construction -> electrics -> plumbing/pumps/filters -> mosaic/marble -> handover; the family firm's full in-house scope is its differentiator vs. Istanbul firms that ship modules.
- **Named, located project cards** (Barr + Wray): every project = city, year, type, 1 sentence, 3-6 photos. Avoid Antonovich-style "project-49".
- **Architect-presented short video per project** (Sagist): a 60-90 s vertical video by the owner/master on site; cheap and personal.
- **Small sizing tool** (Effe): "enter room size -> recommended steam generator / pump / filter class and rough timeline"; a natural lead capture.
- **Gated guide** (Effe eBooks): "How to plan a home hammam" PDF in UZ/RU in exchange for a phone number.
- **WhatsApp + Telegram as primary CTA** (Turkey/Gulf pattern), with one simple form; keep one consistent response-time promise (Antonovich shows the cost of contradicting yourself).
- **Languages UZ / RU / EN** (Antonovich, HFC show RU demand).
- **Hygiene as differentiator**: valid SSL, renewed domain, no template leftovers, no keyword-stuffed headings.

### Gaps
- No verified site offered 360 tours, before/after sliders or a full 3D configurator, so these ideas are not backed by an existing hammam-industry example; they would be original.
- Visual "wow" (hero video, motion, typography) was not observed directly; a screenshot pass is recommended before final design decisions.

# Leading full-scope spa & wellness contractors / premium equipment makers and their websites (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: homepages were fetched on 2026-11-09 with WebFetch (text/markdown extraction) and with curl to read the HTML source for CMS fingerprints (generator meta tags, asset paths, hreflang). WebFetch returns text, not pixels, so "hero" descriptions are based on the headings, slider markup and media references in the page. They are not visual screenshots. Where a site blocked fetching or redirected strangely, that is recorded as a finding or a gap.

## Who are the top players and what scope do they deliver? Notable projects

### Takeaway
The field splits into (a) **full-scope pool + spa design-build contractors** (Barr + Wray, Thermarium, Dale Sauna, Schletterer, Gulf fit-out firms like Antonovich) that deliver design, engineering, supply/installation and after-sales; and (b) **premium manufacturers** (KLAFS, Effe/Effegibi, Starpool, Tylö/Helo, Gharieni, Unbescheiden, Dornbracht) that sell products through a planning/partner network, and some of which also build complete spas. Barr + Wray has the strongest hotel portfolio (Four Seasons, Mandarin Oriental, Ritz-Carlton), and KLAFS has absorbed Thermarium.

## Notable competitors in Uzbekistan / Kazakhstan / Russia with good websites

### Takeaway
I found no Uzbek or Kazakh turnkey hammam/sauna/pool builder with a credible standalone website. The local market runs on OLX and Kaspi listings and messengers. In Russia, the visible players are small firms on Tilda-style landing pages (FinPar) or names known only from advertorials (Stroyhamam, ART-HAMAM, Forma Vody, ITS Sauna). The bar in the CIS region is low.

## Which sites are impressive vs weak, and concrete ideas a small firm can adopt

### Takeaway
A small firm can't match KLAFS's budget. It can still borrow the best mechanics: Barr + Wray's proof-first portfolio and qualifying form, Effe's tools and gated guides, Tylö's configurator idea, and Gharieni's multi-step brief. It can also beat the CIS norm with craft storytelling and messenger-first contact.

### Cited Findings
- Sector-split navigation (residential vs commercial or hospitality) is used by KLAFS and Starpool. — [KLAFS](https://www.klafs.com/?lgchange=1); [Starpool](https://www.starpool.com/en)
- Barr + Wray's qualifying "book a call" form segments leads by Spas / Pools / Aftersales. — [Barr + Wray](https://barrandwray.com/)
- Effe's sizing configurator and gated PDF buying guides, including "how to convert a shower into a hammam". — [Effe](https://www.effe.it/en-us/)
- Tylö's 3D design tool and its trust numbers ("1 million saunas"). — [Tylö](https://tylo.com/)
- Gharieni's multi-step lead form (sector, role, enquiry type, referral) and its after-sales service form. — [Gharieni](https://gharieni.com/)
- Dale Sauna's long list of thermal experiences (hammam, Rasul, salt, ice, loungers) as individual pages, which is good for SEO, plus client testimonials. — [Dale Sauna](https://www.dalesauna.co.uk/)
- A manufacturer's buyer guide gives typical hammam build times of 3–8 weeks for a private home and up to 12 weeks for a large project. It is useful content for a "process" page. This came from a search-result summary and I did not open the guide itself. — [Search summary; vendor guide, unverified](https://dubai.dubizzle.com/community/home-maintenance/interior-design-and-architects/2026/4/11/sauna-steam-room-hammam-design-manufacturi-2-754---c53aad6ac8c84d389fe0233e2e78b9d5/)

### Inferences (ideas to adopt)
1. Project-first homepage. Use full-bleed photos or video of finished hammams, saunas and pools, each card with location, area in m², build time and scope, like Barr + Wray's cards but richer.
2. Show the build process. Use before / under-construction / after sliders and time-lapse. No leader shows the build itself, which is the family firm's real differentiator (marble work, domes, waterproofing).
3. A simple "spa planner". Pick zones (hammam / sauna / pool / relax), enter an area and city, and get an indicative package and timeline. This follows Tylö's 3D configurator and Effe's sizing tool, scaled down.
4. A qualifying multi-step brief (Gharieni / Barr + Wray style) that ends in a Telegram/WhatsApp handoff, the CIS norm per FinPar and Antonovich.
5. Packages with "from" prices (FinPar), plus gated PDF guides in Uzbek/Russian, e.g. "how to plan a hammam in a private house" (Effe).
6. Trilingual uz/ru/en with proper hreflang (KLAFS, Gharieni, Unbescheiden).
7. A service/after-sales section and a maintenance form (Barr + Wray, Gharieni). It signals long-term reliability.
8. Avoid the weak patterns seen: mailto-only CTAs (Dale), no form (Unbescheiden), typos and superlatives (Antonovich, FinPar), broken redirects (Starpool, KLAFS), expired or blocked sites (Thermarium, effegibi.it).

### Gaps
- No usability metrics (Core Web Vitals, conversion rates) were collected for these sites.
- 360/VR tours: none was confirmed on any analysed site. It is an open opportunity rather than a proven pattern.

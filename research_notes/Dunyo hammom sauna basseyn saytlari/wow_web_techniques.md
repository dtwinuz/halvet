# Award-winning spa / wellness / sauna / pool / architecture websites and their "wow" web techniques (as of 2026-11-09)

Scope note: research done with web search + direct fetches of Awwwards `/sites/` pages. Awwwards pages were fetched and read directly where the URL is awwwards.com; other award claims come from agency case studies or a third-party blog and are marked as such. About 20 tool calls were used. Big gap: I could NOT confirm any Awwwards/FWA/CSSDA award for the famous bathhouse brands (Bathhouse NYC, AIRE Ancient Baths, Therme, Loyly Helsinki, 7132 Vals). Do not describe them as "award-winning websites".

## Q1. Which award-recognized sites exist in these categories (exact URLs + award page)?

### Takeaway
Spa and sauna brands rarely win top awards (SOTD). Verified recognitions are mostly Awwwards Honorable Mentions (Leil Saunas, Antara Spa Wellness, Meritage Resort & Spa) and Nominees (Heritage Saunas). The real "Site of the Day" winners that fit the brief are architecture, real-estate and resort sites (Vita Architecture, Explore Primland, Hubtown, ERA). A builder can borrow from both groups: sauna brands for the product and configurator, architecture sites for the cinematic or 3D storytelling.

### Inferences
- A builder ("we build hammams/saunas/pools") sits between the two groups above. The **LEIL/Heritage formula** (luxury minimal, dark earthy palette, craftsmanship storytelling, product configurator) has been recognized on Awwwards *and* produces leads. The **Vita/Primland formula** (WebGL shaders, cinematic camera) earns SOTD but costs far more.
- Dark-green and cream palettes recur in wellness winners (LEIL #313C2B/#EFECE1, Antara #425e40). That makes them a cliché to avoid if the aim is "unique". A hammam-specific palette (marble white, copper, steam grey, Uzbek tile blue) would stand out.

- FWA and Webby searches returned no spa or wellness winners. thefwa.com was not searched directly.
- Only 8 to 10 relevant sites were verified, short of the 10 to 15 target. The Awwwards on-site search for "sauna", "pool" and "bath" (logged-in filtering) was not done.

## Q2. What techniques do they use, and which libraries?

### Takeaway
The common winning stack in 2025-2026 is **Three.js/WebGL + GSAP + Lenis smooth scroll**, usually on Nuxt/React, or Webflow/WordPress for smaller brands. Sauna and spa winners mostly use **parallax, scroll storytelling, page transitions, a configurator and strong photography**. Heavy WebGL (water, fog, terrain flythrough) shows up mainly on SOTD-level architecture and resort sites.

### Cited Findings
- LEIL: WordPress + JS, storytelling, interaction design, product configurator. — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/leil-saunas)
- Heritage Saunas: Webflow, scrolling, unusual navigation, storytelling, configurator. — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/heritage-saunas)
- Antara: animation, parallax, scrolling, transitions, storytelling. — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/antara-spa-wellness)
- Meritage: photographic, storytelling, content architecture, Nuxt.js. — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/meritage-resort-and-spa)
- Vita Architecture: WebGL + shaders + micro-interactions + video. — [Dutch Digital](https://www.dutchdigital.design/cases/vita-architecture)
- Primland: Three.js terrain + **atmospheric fog** + scroll camera flythrough (a steam/fog look). Hubtown: Three.js + GSAP + mouse reveal. Cartier: GLSL + GSAP + Lenis + Web Audio sound design. IVRESS: WebGPU renderer with WebGL fallback using TSL shaders. — [Utsubo](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/best-threejs-websites-2026)
- The stack pattern "Three.js, Nuxt, GSAP and smooth Lenis interactions" appears on SOTD Agrumea Farm. — [Awwwards: Agrumea Farm](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/agrumea-farm)
- **Water/caustics building blocks:**
  - Evan Wallace's method for real-time WebGL caustics. — [Evan Wallace, Rendering Realtime Caustics in WebGL](https://medium.com/@evanwallace/2a99a29a0b2c)
  - A three.js port with ray-traced reflection, refraction and caustics. — [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/real-time-water-simulation-with-three-js)
  - Martin Renou's three.js caustics. — [Medium](https://medium.com/@martinRenou/real-time-rendering-of-water-caustics-59cda1d74aa)
  - Caustics with React Three Fiber shaders. — [Maxime Heckel](https://blog.maximeheckel.com/posts/caustics-in-webgl)
  - The ready-made `Caustics` helper in @pmndrs drei-vanilla. — [pmndrs docs](https://docsearch.algolia.com/mcp/docs/repo/pmndrs/drei-vanilla)
- Awwwards also catalogues reusable water elements, for example "Water shader in three.js", "Accelerated WebGL fluid simulation" and an underwater convex effect. — [Awwwards: water shader](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/water-shader-in-three-js), [fluid simulation](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/accelerated-webgl-fluid-simulation)

### Technique catalogue (cost, complexity, mobile)
Cost and complexity below are my estimates from the sources above and standard practice, not sourced figures.

| Technique | Seen on | Complexity / cost | Mobile behaviour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photographic + storytelling scroll, parallax, page transitions (GSAP + Lenis) | Antara, Meritage, Heritage | Low to medium | Good if parallax is reduced. Lenis smooth scroll can feel odd on touch, so use native scroll on mobile |
| Product configurator (pick size, wood, stove, options, then send) | LEIL, Heritage | Medium (2D image layers) to high (3D) | 2D layered images work well. 3D needs LOD and fallbacks |
| Video hero (steam, water, fire) | Vita (video), common in spa | Low | Needs a poster image, muted autoplay with `playsinline` and a small mobile encode. Slow loads can hurt conversion |
| WebGL water / caustics shader over a pool or hammam image | Awwwards water elements, Evan Wallace demo | Medium to high (custom GLSL) | Run at reduced resolution on mobile or swap to a static image or video |
| Steam / fog shader or atmospheric fog in a 3D scene | Primland fog | Medium to high | Fog is cheap in three.js, while volumetric steam is expensive. Use a canvas or CSS fallback |
| Scroll-driven 3D camera flythrough (a model of the hammam or dome) | Primland, Oryzo, Hubtown | High (3D modelling + optimisation) | Risky on mid-range phones. Needs a lighter fallback scene |
| Mouse-reveal / cursor effects | Hubtown, Vita micro-interactions | Low to medium | Do nothing on touch, so tap or scroll alternatives are needed |
| Sound design (Web Audio) | Cartier | Low to medium | Must be opt-in. Autoplay audio is blocked anyway |
| WebGPU with WebGL fallback | IVRESS | High | This is a pattern for robustness |

### Inferences
- A Central Asian hammam/sauna builder could get a unique "wow" from one **signature shader** tied to its product: a steam veil that clears on scroll to reveal the dome interior, or caustic light on the pool floor. It would sit on top of a photography-led, configurator-backed site, which avoids building a full 3D world.

### Gaps
- I did not inspect the actual JS bundles of LEIL, Heritage or Antara, so their library choices (GSAP or Lenis) are not confirmed beyond the Awwwards tags.
- No Codrops tutorial specifically on caustics was found. Codrops search was not done directly.
- 360 tours were not found on any of the verified award sites.

## Q3. Which techniques convert (leads) vs just impress? Evidence?

### Takeaway
Configurators have the best (but vendor-reported) lead evidence: roughly +30% to 4x leads. Immersive WebGL has evidence for **engagement** (Vita: bounce down 50%, sessions 5x longer) but not for leads. Hero video has **no reliable evidence** of conversion lift, and load time can make it hurt.

### Cited Findings
- **F.Domes** (dome structures, which is close to "gumbaz"): a 3D configurator raised **monthly qualified leads by 30 to 40%**. — [Sketchfab case study](https://sketchfab.com/blogs/enterprise/3d-configurators/manufacturing-fdomes)
- **Leitl Garages** configurator (Combeenation, vendor-reported): +30% inquiries, -50% quotation time, +40% site hits. — [Combeenation](https://combeenation.com/en/blogs/details/best-case-inquiry-configurator)
- **KLAFS sauna configurator / sauna finder** (Combeenation) was built for lead generation. No measured results were published. — [Combeenation sauna case](https://www.combeenation.com/en/case-studies/sauna-configurator/)
- SaleSqueze (vendor) claims up to 5x qualified leads and +40% sales for outdoor and spa products. It offers a timber sauna 3D configurator product. These are marketing claims with no methodology. — [SaleSqueze timber sauna](https://salesqueze.com/timber-sauna-3d-configurator/), [Mizarstvo Hrovat case](https://www.casestudies.com/company/salesqueze/case-study/modular-houses-manufacturer-digitalizes-sales-133m-of-leads-in-30-months)
- **Ellio** quadrupled inbound leads with a ConvertCalculator product configurator (vendor case). — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/convertcalculator/case-study/how-ellio-4xd-inbound-leads-with-a-product-configurator)
- **Presidential Pools**: 4x leads and 2 to 3x close rate. The driver was a CRM and marketing automation system (Keap), not a website effect. — [casestudies.com](https://www.casestudies.com/company/keap/case-study/presidential-pools-quadruples-lead-generation)
- **Bullfrog Spas** interactive dealer lead tool: leads "increased ... in drastic margins", with no numbers given. — [Aqua Magazine](https://www.aquamagazine.com/retail/article/15122043/bullfrog-launches-interactive-leadgen-tool)
- **Vita Architecture** WebGL site: bounce -50%, session 30 s to 2 min 30 s, about 40k visitors at launch. These are engagement metrics, not leads. — [Dutch Digital](https://www.dutchdigital.design/cases/vita-architecture)
- **Hero video**:
  - One test log shows +19.4% e-commerce conversion with a muted looping video hero, while another homepage test found no significant difference. — [abtestbot](https://abtestbot.pages.dev/ab-testing-for/homepage)
  - Food Blogger Pro suspected its video hurt conversion through load time. — [Food Blogger Pro](https://www.foodbloggerpro.com/blog/how-we-improved-our-landing-page-conversion-rate-by-138/)
  - An animated gradient instead of heavy media improved LCP by 1.1 s with similar conversion. — [Stellar](https://gostellar.app/blog/high-impact-hero-sections-that-dont-hurt-page-speed)
- Pool & Spa News runs a "Top 50 Builder" website program framed around turning curious homeowners into hot leads. Not fetched. — [Pool & Spa News](https://www.poolspanews.com/?p=35576)

### Inferences
- For a small builder, the best return order is likely: (1) a **configurator or price calculator** ("choose hammam size, dome, marble, steam generator, then get an estimate on WhatsApp or Telegram"), (2) a strong photo or video portfolio of real projects, (3) one signature visual effect for memorability. A full 3D world mostly impresses.

### Gaps
- No independent, non-vendor study of configurator impact for home-improvement builders was found. Every lead figure above is self-reported.
- No data on Uzbekistan or the CIS market.

## Q4. Pitfalls: performance, mobile, accessibility

### Takeaway
The main risks are heavy WebGL or video on mid-range Android phones and in-app browsers (Telegram, Instagram), slow LCP that kills conversion, and motion that ignores accessibility. Award-site write-ups rarely publish mobile data, so plan fallbacks from day one.

### Cited Findings
- Recommended practice for Three.js sites: test on mid-range phones and **in-app browsers**, not only desktop GPUs. Give weaker devices a lighter fallback. Use instancing, baked lighting, BVH collision and per-scene byte budgets. None of the 8 SOTD sites in that roundup published mobile performance data. — [Utsubo](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/best-threejs-websites-2026)
- IVRESS shows the robustness pattern of WebGPU with automatic WebGL fallback. — [Utsubo](https://www.utsubo.com/blog/best-threejs-websites-2026)
- Video heroes can cut conversions when they slow page load. — [Food Blogger Pro](https://www.foodbloggerpro.com/blog/how-we-improved-our-landing-page-conversion-rate-by-138/). Lighter animated backgrounds improved LCP by 1.1 s. — [Stellar](https://gostellar.app/blog/high-impact-hero-sections-that-dont-hurt-page-speed)

### Inferences
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and give static fallbacks for shaders. Keep text real HTML, not canvas, so it stays crawlable and accessible. Make sure CTAs (call, Telegram, WhatsApp, the configurator) are reachable without waiting for the 3D to load. Use opt-in sound only. Lenis-style smooth scroll should fall back to native scroll on touch devices.
- Uzbek audiences often arrive from Telegram or Instagram in-app browsers on mid-range Android phones. That makes mobile fallback a top requirement rather than a nice-to-have. This is an inference and was not measured.

### Gaps
- No sourced WCAG or accessibility audit of any listed award site was found.
- No measured Core Web Vitals for LEIL, Heritage, Antara or Vita were collected. A Lighthouse or PageSpeed run would close this.

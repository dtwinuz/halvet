# Leading luxury pool builders and premium pool-mosaic brands: websites as marketing platforms (state as of Nov 2026)

Method note: every homepage below was fetched live on 2026-11-09 through a text-extracting fetcher. The fetcher returns page text and markup hints, not rendered visuals. Claims about motion, video and visual polish are therefore limited to what the markup shows (slider plugins, "re-watch intro" links, video placeholders). Three intended targets could not be verified and were left out of the main list (see Gaps): Questar Pools (USA, Skip Phillips), Xterior (Dubai), and Carré Bleu (France).

## 1. Who are the top builders/designers (awards, Genesis/Watershape, infinity-edge), notable projects and scope?

### Takeaway
The verifiable leaders split into three kinds. (a) Award-heavy regional custom builders: Laguna Pools (Melbourne, SPASA 2025 national Best Concrete Pool Builder) and California Pools & Landscape (Arizona, PHTA awards; parent Cody Pools was PHTA 2025 Builder of the Year). (b) UK luxury indoor/basement and wellness specialists: Guncast (Klafs partner, 50+ years) and London Swimming Pool Company (since 1984). (c) Italian industrial-scale brands: Piscine Castiglione (200 dealers, projects in 70+ countries, pools plus sauna/steam/Kneipp) and its sister Myrtha Pools (Olympic/World Aquatics pools, 200 world records). In the Gulf, the best-verified luxury site found is Antonovich Group (Dubai), a turnkey design-build firm where pools are a supporting specialty, not the core business.

## 2. Website anatomy for each: URL, CMS, hero, structure, portfolio, tools, languages, lead capture, premium vs dated

### Takeaway
Most leading builders run conventional WordPress sites: a slider or photo hero, a long scrolling homepage, and a project grid. They win on proof (awards, real projects, process) rather than interactive technology. The interactive standouts are Castiglione's pool configurator plus its "dream pool" explorer, Cal Pools' Dreambook app and 3D design, and Laguna's detailed project-brief form. The UK sites are the most restrained and premium in tone, and Dingman is the dated, SEO-template example.

### Cited Findings
- **Laguna** (WordPress: wp-content paths and a visible WP admin error). Hero is a 2-slide banner (SPASA award and "Pools with Purpose") over "AWARD-WINNING CUSTOM CONCRETE POOL BUILDERS IN MELBOURNE". The single long scroll runs: awards → services → portfolio → trusted brands → build process → **live build updates** → press logos → guarantees → testimonials → forms → offices → FAQ → news. Each project card shows pool type and size. It documents a family build as an ongoing story. Lead capture has **two forms: a quick enquiry and a detailed brief** (build type, budget, site access, timeline, plan upload up to 10MB) with a "free design consultation" offer. There is no calculator or configurator. One flaw: the news feed displayed a token-decryption error, a visible bug — [Laguna](https://www.lagunapools.com.au/)
- **California Pools** (WordPress plus RevSlider). Hero has the headline and a "Request an Estimate" button, with no video detected. Mega-menus cover products and about 20 service-area cities. Tools: the **Dreambook 2.0 app**, "advanced 3D modeling" for design, and a **7-step financing application**. Sticky CTA bars read "GET AN ESTIMATE / MEET A DESIGNER / GET FUNDED". It has a gallery of about 30 photos and an "End of the Year Sale" banner — [Cal Pools](https://www.calpool.com/)
- **Guncast** (built by Molokini Marketing). Hero has a "Re-watch intro" link, which points to an intro video. Five entry tiles: Pools, Wellness Suites, Commercial, Aftercare, Portfolio. Lead capture: request a callback, a brochure, design-consultancy booking, and printed catalogues by post (wellness, pool, or both). It also has an **Architect Zone**, a YouTube build series and an Instagram feed — [Guncast](https://www.guncast.com/)
- **London Swimming Pool Co** (probably ProcessWire, inferred from /site/templates/ paths). The hero is a photo slideshow (not video) with four one-word slides: "Luxury / Indulgence / Inspiration / Individuality". The portfolio grid filters Construction vs Refurbishment. There are no interactive tools. The form has an "I'm interested in" dropdown, plus a brochure download — [LSPC](https://www.londonswimmingpools.com/)
- **Piscine Castiglione** (WordPress, probably Ninja Forms). The hero is a 2026 promo banner with a cookie-gated video. The mega-menu sorts pools by style, location, sfioro/skimmer, colour, finishes and accessories. Tools: a **"Com'è la Piscina dei Tuoi Sogni?" six-path tabbed explorer** and **"Configura la tua Piscina Ideale"** (style, filtration, colour, finishes, accessories). Lead capture: dealer locator, **showroom booking**, a **free ebook "Progettare una Piscina" in exchange for an email**, a catalogue request, and FAQ by topic (rules, costs, design, maintenance). The site is in Italian only — [Castiglione](https://www.piscinecastiglione.it/)
- **Myrtha Pools** (built by Basilico, Cookiebot). It has a region selector (APAC/EMEA/Americas) and 5 languages (EN/FR/IT/ES/DE). Project enquiry buttons appear throughout. It has no configurator — [Myrtha](https://www.myrthapools.com/)
- **Antonovich Group** (template CMS). The hero carries a Forbes badge and buttons for Book Consultation, WhatsApp and phone. The long scroll includes a portfolio filtered by discipline and a 5-step process. Lead capture is aggressive: a free-consultation popup, a floating "Get a Quote" button, **"AI Chat"**, and repeated WhatsApp links. Its promised response times contradict each other ("within 1 hour" vs "within one business day"). Languages: **EN/AR/RU** — [Antonovich](https://antonovich-design.ae/)
- **Dingman Pools** (WordPress plus Slider Revolution). The slider repeats the same banner on every slide, it has a long list of town links for SEO, and the footer copyright reads 2022 — [Dingman](https://dingmanpools.com/)

### Inferences
- The common premium pattern is: one-line positioning → award or proof strip → 3–5 service tiles → curated projects with specs → process explanation → guarantees → two-tier lead form.
- Weaker sites show it through visible bugs, outdated copyright years, repeated banners and SEO town lists.
- No builder site shows an engineering cutaway (pumps, filters, balance tank) or a before/after slider, so these are open differentiators.

### Gaps
- The fetcher cannot render the pages, so typography, motion quality, WebGL use and real hero media could not be judged visually. A browser pass with screenshots is needed.
- Page speed and Core Web Vitals were not measured.

## 3. Mosaic brands: online configurators/visualizers and how they work

### Takeaway
The Spanish glass-mosaic makers lead on tools. Ezarri has a "water color simulator" that mixes up to 4 references with a grout colour and leads to a quote request or downloadable file. Hisbalit's mixtures configurator handles mixes and gradients with exact percentages, 4 shapes, grout and surface finish, and ends in a download, a saved design and a physical sample. The Italian luxury brands (Bisazza, Sicis) sell through bespoke design services rather than pool tools. Bisazza only has a general "Configure your space" app.

### Cited Findings
- **Ezarri** (https://www.ezarri.com/en): positioned as a "Recycled glass mosaic manufacturer for swimming pools, wellness, and interior design". Menus offer a 3D Configurator, a **mosaic calculator** for professionals, Jointpoint® installation ("25% faster installation"), and a gallery filtered by *water colour* and use. Languages: ES/EU/EN/FR/PT/DE/US. Tagline: "Dreams are made of mosaics" — [Ezarri](https://www.ezarri.com/en)
- **Ezarri configurator** (/en/configurator): choose mosaics → choose joint colour → "mix up to 4 references to see a simulation of the final result" → "request a quote and download a file of your project". It calls itself a "water color simulator", and some references cannot be mixed — [Ezarri configurator](https://www.ezarri.com/en/configurator)
- **Europe Imports** (Ezarri's Australian distributor) runs a Pool Tile Visualiser: choose a pool scene (one live, two "coming soon") → pick an Ezarri mosaic, mosaic grout, a stone or porcelain surround and the surround grout → preview → "Ready to Make it Real?" quote — [Europe Imports](https://www.europeimports.com.au/design-your-dream-pool)
- **Hisbalit** mixtures configurator (https://www.hisbalit.es/en/mixtures-configurator). The page is JS-rendered, so the steps come from a press description:
  - Process: mix or gradient → colours (200+, up to 5 recommended, percentage per colour) → shape (circles, hexagons, 2.5×2.5 or 4×4 cm squares) → grout → generate → view on a surface → adjust → download or save
  - Surfaces: glossy, anti-slip B or C (for pools and wellness)
  - Pricing tiers: OPEN MIX and MIX 15. A physical sample is made before ordering, and delivery takes about 3 weeks
  - Hisbalit also lists "Color finder", "Simulators" and "Art factory · Personalization" (custom image mosaics)
  - Sources: [Archiweb press piece](https://www.archiweb.cz/en/n/press/namichejte-si-osobni-mozaiku); [Hisbalit](https://www.hisbalit.es/en/mixtures-configurator); [ArchDaily on Art Factory](https://www.archdaily.com/catalog/us/products/31004/how-to-personalise-ceramic-tiles-with-art-factory-hisbalit)
- **Bisazza** (https://www.bisazza.com/, "Website by Drop", Cookiebot). It links a "Configure your space" app at app.bisazza.com. The Design Studio offers free consultation on reproducing a drawing or photo in mosaic. There is no pool section. Languages: EN/IT plus 24 country sites — [Bisazza](https://www.bisazza.com/)
- **Sicis** (https://www.sicis.com/, Ravenna). The hero slider includes "WELLNESS BEYOND THE SPA: Mosaic, Vetrite and bespoke solutions for spaces dedicated to wellbeing". Navigation is by audience (Developers, Architects, Private Clients). It has a Design Service but no configurator and no pool section. Only EN is visible — [Sicis](https://www.sicis.com/)

### Inferences
- A small builder can copy the Hisbalit/Ezarri logic without a tile maker's catalogue: a pool-scene preview where the visitor picks mosaic colour, grout and surround, sees the water tint change, and sends the combination with a quote request.
- Water colour depends mainly on the mosaic colour, and Ezarri's gallery filter by water colour shows customers think this way.

### Gaps
- Trend Group was not checked within the tool budget.
- The internals of the Ezarri and Hisbalit tools (true 3D/WebGL vs 2D compositing) could not be confirmed from text.
- What app.bisazza.com actually does was not examined.

## 4. Which sites are genuinely impressive (Awwwards/CSSDA/FWA) and which are weak

### Takeaway
No pool builder site with an Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD) was found. The only pool-company Awwwards entry located is a 2023 Nominee with a mediocre community score. Excellence in this niche comes from content and proof, not from award-winning web technology, so a well-crafted site would stand out globally.

### Cited Findings
- **Dingman Pools** on Awwwards: Nominee only, 6 Mar 2023, submitted by Benjamin Marc. 15 voters gave scores of 4.00–6.40, an average of about 5.56/10 — [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/dingman-pools). The live site is a conventional WordPress and Slider Revolution template — [Dingman](https://dingmanpools.com/)
- Related Awwwards entries are water-themed rather than pool builders: a hotel pool page by Spoon for Røroshotellene, "Water by Falmec", and "The Sea We Breathe" (SOTD 15 Nov 2021) — [Røroshotellene](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/swimming-pool-roroshotellene); [Falmec](https://awwwards.com/sites/water-by-falmec); [The Sea We Breathe](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/the-sea-we-breathe)
- **Strongest as marketing platforms** (judged on content and function):
  - Laguna: depth of proof, live build updates, a detailed brief form — [Laguna](https://www.lagunapools.com.au/)
  - Castiglione: two guided design tools, an ebook lead magnet, a showroom — [Castiglione](https://www.piscinecastiglione.it/)
  - Guncast: intro film, Architect Zone, printed catalogue — [Guncast](https://www.guncast.com/)
  - Cal Pools: app, 3D design, financing funnel — [Cal Pools](https://www.calpool.com/)
- **Weak signals:**
  - Dingman: repeated banner, 2022 copyright, SEO town lists — [Dingman](https://dingmanpools.com/)
  - Laguna: a broken news-feed error shown publicly — [Laguna](https://www.lagunapools.com.au/)
  - Antonovich: contradictory response-time promises — [Antonovich](https://antonovich-design.ae/)
  - LSPC: "award-winning" with no named awards — [LSPC](https://www.londonswimmingpools.com/)

### Inferences
- The bar for "the most impressive pool website in the world" is low on the technical side. A tasteful scroll-driven water/WebGL hero, real drone film and an engineering explainer would already exceed every builder site checked.

### Gaps
- CSSDA and FWA were not searched. An Awwwards search filtered to "pool" or "spa" could still turn up winners missed here.

## 5. Concrete ideas a small Uzbek firm could adopt

### Takeaway
Combine the proof stack of Laguna/Guncast with the guided tools of Castiglione/Hisbalit, add the engineering transparency no competitor shows, and localise for Uzbek, Russian and English with WhatsApp/Telegram contact (the Dubai pattern).

### Cited Findings (each idea is traced to the site where it appears)
- **Three or four entry tiles** (Pools / Hammam / Sauna / Service), as in Guncast's five tiles — [Guncast](https://www.guncast.com/)
- **A "dream pool" explorer and configurator**: style → skimmer vs overflow (infinity) → mosaic colour → finishes → equipment, following Castiglione — [Castiglione](https://www.piscinecastiglione.it/)
- **A mosaic and water-colour preview**: mix up to 4 colours plus grout, see the tinted water, send it with a quote request (Ezarri / Hisbalit / Europe Imports logic) — [Ezarri configurator](https://www.ezarri.com/en/configurator); [Archiweb on Hisbalit](https://www.archiweb.cz/en/n/press/namichejte-si-osobni-mozaiku); [Europe Imports](https://www.europeimports.com.au/design-your-dream-pool)
- **Project cards with specs** (type, size, mosaic, equipment) and **live build diaries**, as Laguna does — [Laguna](https://www.lagunapools.com.au/)
- **A two-tier lead form**: a quick call request plus a detailed brief with site photo/plan upload, budget and timeline, as Laguna does — [Laguna](https://www.lagunapools.com.au/)
- **A lead magnet**: a free guide ("How to plan a pool / hammam") in exchange for contact details, like Castiglione's ebook — [Castiglione](https://www.piscinecastiglione.it/)
- **A visible process and guarantees block** (upfront quote, concept to completion), as Laguna and Antonovich have — [Laguna](https://www.lagunapools.com.au/); [Antonovich](https://antonovich-design.ae/)
- **Multilingual pages plus messenger CTA**: Antonovich runs EN/AR/RU with WhatsApp. For Uzbekistan the same pattern would be UZ/RU/EN plus Telegram — [Antonovich](https://antonovich-design.ae/)
- **Aftercare as a revenue page** (service, winterising, water treatment), as Castiglione, Guncast and LSPC do — [Castiglione](https://www.piscinecastiglione.it/); [Guncast](https://www.guncast.com/); [LSPC](https://www.londonswimmingpools.com/)
- **An architect/partner zone**, as Guncast has — [Guncast](https://www.guncast.com/)
- **Avoid** stale dates, repeated banners, broken widgets, unnamed "award-winning" claims and contradictory SLAs — [Dingman](https://dingmanpools.com/); [Laguna](https://www.lagunapools.com.au/); [Antonovich](https://antonovich-design.ae/)

### Inferences
- The clearest gap among all sites checked is an **engineering cutaway**: an interactive diagram of pump → filter → heater → dosing → overflow/balance tank, plus the hammam steam generator and the sauna heater. No builder shows one, and it directly proves the client's turnkey engineering scope.
- A **before/after slider** for renovations and an **indicative cost range** (Dubai firms publish rough price tiers on blogs) are cheap to build and missing from the premium sites — [Al Naseem blog (low-quality source, cost tiers only)](https://alnaseempools.blogspot.com/2026/04/top-expert-swimming-pool-construction.html)

### Gaps
- No market data on which of these features actually raises conversion was found. Their value is inferred from the fact that leading firms use them.

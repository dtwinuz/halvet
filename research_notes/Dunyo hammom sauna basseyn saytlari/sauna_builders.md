# World-leading sauna builders, design studios and premium sauna brands: their websites as marketing platforms (as of Nov 2026)

Method note: homepages were fetched with a text-extracting fetcher on 2026-11-09. It reads HTML text and asset paths, not rendered visuals or client-side JS. So hero media type, WebGL and configurators are only confirmed where text or markup shows them. Where Awwwards describes a feature the fetch did not see, both are reported. Platform/CMS is inferred from asset paths (`wp-content`, `/cdn/shop/`) unless the site credits a builder.

## Q1. Who are the top players, and who builds turnkey vs only sells products?

### Takeaway
The strongest names fall into three groups:
- **Product makers that sell through dealers:** Harvia, Narvi, Tylö/Helo (Sauna360), Saunum, Auroom, Leil.
- **Full-service planners/builders:** KLAFS (Germany) is the reference case at the top end.
- **Regional bespoke design-build firms:** Heritage Saunas (NZ), Finnmark Sauna (UK), Out of the Valley (UK), Southeast Leisure (US).

The bespoke builders are the closest model for a small turnkey hammam/sauna/pool firm.

### Inferences
- Large brands (Harvia, Tylö, Narvi) sell products and push visitors to a dealer. Their websites are catalogs plus brand storytelling.
- Turnkey marketing (process, references, consultation) is done well by KLAFS at the top end and by regional builders (Heritage, Finnmark).
- For a small Uzbek turnkey firm, the best models are KLAFS's "Planning + Private References" structure and Heritage Saunas' craft storytelling, not the heater makers' catalogs.
- KLAFS sends Central Asian visitors to klafs.kz. That suggests KLAFS is present in Kazakhstan, which matters for the competitive landscape. This is unverified beyond the redirect.

### Gaps

- No Australian homepage was verified.
- Out of the Valley's homepage was down (522) at fetch time.

## Q2. Website anatomy of each company (URL, platform, hero, structure, portfolio, interactivity, languages, lead capture, premium vs dated)

### Takeaway
Most sauna sites are WordPress (with WPML) or Shopify. They use static or photo heroes, card grids and "find a dealer" CTAs. Real interactivity is rare. The interactive features found were:
- Tylö's desktop-only 3D designer
- KLAFS's sauna finder and (per its vendor) a 3D/AR configurator
- Calculators at Saunum and Narvi
- Auroom's virtual showroom

### Cited Findings

**Harvia** (https://www.harvia.com/en/)
- Hero: full-width image with "Let's sauna."
- Structure: Fenix smart-control spotlight, then Sauna / Infrared / Steam & Spa cards, an animated stats counter band, sustainability, product highlights (Cilindro, Spirit, Frosty cold plunge), an "Ideas & Trends" health content hub, a dealer locator.
- No configurator or calculator on the homepage.
- Feel: premium whitespace, but footer typos ("Sustainabililty", "Vulnerablity").
- [Harvia](https://www.harvia.com/en/)

**Tylö** (https://www.tylo.com/)
- Hero: image linking to a video, "Redefining wellness / For the life you live / For the life you want".
- Has a "Design in 3D" configurator, marked desktop-only, and a blog post promoting designing a sauna in the browser.
- 8 language/market variants.
- Lead capture: "Keep me posted" email signup, dealer finder.
- Rough edges: malformed blog URL `/blog/en/blog/`.
- [Tylö](https://www.tylo.com/)

**Narvi** (https://www.narvi.fi/)

### Inferences
- The two sites with design-award recognition (Leil, Heritage) share four traits: a video or immersive hero with a short poetic line, storytelling scroll, materials and ritual content, and one persistent enquiry CTA. Neither is a crowded catalog.

### Gaps
- Visual details (fonts, colour, hero video vs still, WebGL) could not be verified with a text fetch. A real browser session or screenshots are needed.
- I found no CSSDA or FWA awards for sauna sites. The only search done was an Awwwards one, so this is not exhaustive.

## Q3. Which sites are genuinely impressive, and which are weak?

### Takeaway
The most impressive, with third-party recognition, are **Leil Saunas** (Awwwards Honorable Mention, May 2026) and **Heritage Saunas** (Awwwards Nominee, Jul 2026).

- **KLAFS** is the content benchmark (planning plus references plus a configurator).
- **Tylö** has a strong tool, though it is desktop-only.
- **Weak:** Southeast Leisure, and Narvi and Saunum on execution details.

  - Lanco Saunas (Pennsylvania), in Made in Webflow. [Webflow](https://webflow.com/made-in-webflow/website/lanco-saunas-8fc43f)
  - A Framer template for sauna studios, "Quench". [Framer](https://framer.com/templates/quench?via=framax)
- Weak points:
  - Narvi shows a raw form shortcode. [Narvi](https://www.narvi.fi/)
  - Saunum has placeholder help links. [Saunum](https://www.saunum.com/)

## Q4. Concrete ideas a small Uzbek turnkey hammam/sauna/pool firm could adopt

### Takeaway
Combine three models:
- **Heritage Saunas' scroll story:** craft, then materials, then ritual, then builds, then "Let's design yours".
- **KLAFS's planning area:** sauna finder questionnaire, a made-to-measure page, a private references library.
- **One light interactive tool:** a size/scale selector or cost calculator.

All of it in uz/ru/en, with flawless execution.

### Cited Findings (patterns to borrow, each seen live)
- **Hero:** short emotional line plus one CTA, as in Heritage ("A sauna, built to last. Made just for you.") and Leil (video, "A mindful pause."). [Heritage](https://heritagesaunas.co.nz/); [Leil](https://leilsaunas.com/)
- **Materials section with named woods and stones** (redwood, hinoki, stone). For a hammam this maps to marble, onyx, tadelakt or ganch, and majolica. [Heritage](https://heritagesaunas.co.nz/)
- **Ritual explainer:** Heritage's "15 min heat / 5 min cold" cycle. [Heritage](https://heritagesaunas.co.nz/)
- **Scale selector with real dimensions plus a "Custom" option**, leading to an on-site consultation. [Heritage](https://heritagesaunas.co.nz/)
- **Guided finder questionnaire:** KLAFS's sauna finder, a few short questions. [KLAFS](https://klafs.com/residential/planning/private-references)
- **Private references library:** one page per project with a story title. [KLAFS refs](https://klafs.com/residential/planning/private-references/detail/shape-sauna-as-an-artistic-experience)
- **One SEO case-study post per install, by neighbourhood.** Finnmark's Hampstead and Brondesbury Park pages are the example; the equivalent would be "Hammam in Yunusobod". [Finnmark](https://finnmarksauna.com/blogs/outdoor-sauna-installations/bespoke-outdoor-sauna-installation-brondesbury-park-north-west-london)
- **Trust signals:**
  - Stats band (Harvia's years and units). [Harvia](https://www.harvia.com/en/)
  - Client logos (Finnmark). [Finnmark](https://finnmarksauna.com/)
  - Press quotes (Heritage). [Heritage](https://heritagesaunas.co.nz/)
- **Calculator as a lead magnet:** Saunum's and Narvi's sauna volume and heater calculators. [Saunum](https://www.saunum.com/); [Narvi](https://www.narvi.fi/)
- **Chat-first lead capture:** Saunum uses a Messenger widget. The local equivalent is a Telegram/WhatsApp button. [Saunum](https://www.saunum.com/)
- **Downloadable catalogue / "reference book" PDF:** Auroom. [Auroom](https://auroomwellness.com/)
- **Virtual showroom:** Auroom's "Enter our virtual showroom". Tylö's 3D designer is an upper bound, but note it is desktop-only. [Auroom](https://auroomwellness.com/); [Tylö](https://www.tylo.com/)

### Inferences
- **Priority 1 is execution hygiene**, because rough edges on otherwise premium sites hurt credibility: no half-translated forms (Auroom), no broken shortcodes (Narvi), no typos (Harvia, SEL).
- **Priority 2 is real project photography with a story per project.** This is the gap even good builders leave (SEL has no gallery; Finnmark shows only tiles).
- **Unique angle no competitor found has:** a combined hammam + sauna + pool turnkey story, and an interactive "heat and steam explainer" that compares hammam (wet, ~45–50°C) with sauna (dry, hot). No site fetched had an animated heat/steam explainer; Saunum explains its tech in text only. The hammam temperature figure is general knowledge and was not sourced in this research.
- **Mobile-first matters:** Tylö's 3D designer being desktop-only is a missed opportunity in a phone-first market like Uzbekistan.

### Gaps
- No data on conversion rates or the effectiveness of configurators vs enquiry forms for sauna firms.
- Pricing transparency practices for turnkey builders were not researched; only Heritage shows "from" prices, and only for heaters.
